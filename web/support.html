Windgate · Help and contact

Windgate is a guided breathing app. Pick a pattern, choose a length, tap Begin, and follow the circle. If something is not working, write to us and a person will answer.

Common questions

How do I make my own pattern? Open the Patterns tab, tap the plus button, name it and set the seconds for breathe in, hold, breathe out and hold.

Where is my history? The Progress tab shows your streak, minutes this week and past sessions. It lives only on your device.

The reminder did not arrive. Open Settings, turn Daily reminder on, and allow notifications for Windgate in your system settings.

No haptics. Haptics need an iPhone or a Mac trackpad. Check the Haptics switch in Settings.
