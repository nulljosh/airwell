Privacy Policy

Windgate · Last updated 10 September 2026

Windgate is a breathing exercise tool. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, stored or sold by us. The app never asks who you are.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Windgate loads its own page and runs entirely in your browser or app. No breathing session, pattern choice, or custom timing is sent anywhere.

What is kept

Your preset and sound settings are kept only on your device, in local storage, so the app remembers your choice next time. Nothing is kept on any server.

Third parties

None. Windgate has no third-party services, trackers, or embedded content of any kind.

Children

Windgate does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it.
