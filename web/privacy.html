Privacy Policy

Airwell · Last updated 3 October 2026

Airwell is a breathing exercise tool. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, stored or sold by us. The app never asks who you are.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps run entirely on your device, and the website tool runs in your browser. No breathing session, pattern, custom timing or history is sent anywhere.

What is kept

Your custom breathing patterns, your session history and streak, and your settings (haptics and the daily reminder time) are kept only on your device. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your device, and it is only set up if you turn it on. Nothing is kept on any server. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third parties

None. Airwell has no third-party services, trackers, or embedded content of any kind.

Children

Airwell does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it.
