Airwell, guided breathing

A quiet minute for your breath.

Four breathing patterns and a circle to follow. No account, no download, just breathe.

The west wind, slowed down

Breathe on a count

A circle grows while you breathe in and shrinks while you breathe out. You only have to follow it.

Patterns

Four patterns, or your own

Box, 4-7-8, Relax and Coherent. Set your own counts for in, hold, out and hold.

On your phone

Felt, not watched

On iPhone a soft tap marks each turn, so you can breathe with your eyes closed.

Habit

A streak

The apps keep your sessions and count the days in a row.

Reminder

One nudge a day

Pick a time. Airwell asks once, then leaves you alone.

Private

Nothing leaves

No account and no tracking. Your history stays on your device.
