FAQ

What is Airwell?

A guided breathing app. You get four patterns (box, 4-7-8, relax and coherent), or your own, and a circle that expands and contracts so you just follow it.

Do I need an account?

No. Airwell has no account and no tracking. Your history stays on your device.

Where does it run?

Free in any browser. There is also an iPhone, iPad and Mac app on the App Store, with a tap on every turn of the breath.
