Show HN: Windgate – a guided breathing app where the circle is the timer

Body:
Windgate is a one-screen breathing tool. Pick a pattern (Box, 4-7-8, Relax, or Coherent) and a circle expands and contracts to match it. The circle's CSS transition is the actual timer, so there's no number counting down and nothing to track but your breath. No account, no backend, no storage beyond the open tab.

It's built with plain HTML, CSS, and a bit of JavaScript, no framework. The whole app is one page. Live at https://windgate.heyitsmejosh.com, free to use. There's also a $0.99 App Store version for people who want it as a standalone app on their phone.

I'd like feedback on the timing feel across patterns and whether the light/dark handling reads right on your system.
